DeckCast
Lecture Deck Assistant

Turn a lecture into a slide deck.

Paste or record a lecture. The agents pull out the key points, build the slides, and check every claim against what was actually said. Then export the deck as a .pptx file.

Everything runs in your browser. Your lecture text and your AI provider key never leave this page except to the provider you choose.

1. Give it a lecture

Example: How Photosynthesis Works. Leave it blank and the first line of the transcript is used.

Paste a transcript

Or record with the microphone, or upload audio

Record with the microphone

Speak the lecture while the page listens. In Chrome or Edge your words are transcribed live and saved as the transcript. In other browsers the recording is transcribed on this device with Whisper after you stop. A recording of the audio is kept on the page so you can play it back.

Your live transcription will appear here while you speak.

Upload an audio file

Uploaded audio is transcribed on this device with Whisper and saved as the transcript. The file is also kept for playback. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

2. Check the transcript

No transcript yet. Paste one above, record with the microphone, or load the sample lecture.

3. Build the deck

Building is deterministic: every bullet comes from a sentence in the transcript, and each one keeps a link to its source sentence so the fact checker can verify it.

The preview below shows the chosen theme, and the .pptx export uses it too. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Ask DeckCast

Ask for a deck, a summary, or a check of the slides. The agents work with the lecture and deck on this page.

Chat is sent to your selected AI provider. Add your own provider key in AI model settings below. Without a key, the assistant runs in demo mode and can still build decks from the page.
