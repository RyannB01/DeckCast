Privacy

Short version: DeckCast collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

Your lecture stays in your browser

Transcripts you paste, audio you record or upload, and the slides DeckCast builds from them are processed in your browser only. They are never uploaded to a server, because DeckCast has no server. Closing the page discards them.

Your AI provider key stays in your browser

The assistant can use a real AI model if you add your own provider key in AI model settings. The key is stored in your browser's local storage only. It is sent only to the provider you choose, and only when you send a chat message. It is never sent to DeckCast, never sent to any peer site, and never put in a URL or a file. Set a spending limit on the key with your provider before you use it.

To remove a saved key, delete it in AI model settings, or clear this site's data in your browser. Either way it is gone from this browser.

Cookies

The deployed site sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. One exception exists only on local development runs: when the site runs on localhost, the runtime mirrors the saved provider profile to a same-site host cookie so settings follow across local ports. That cookie is never set on the deployed site.

Third parties

DeckCast loads no fonts, scripts, styles, images, or analytics from a third party. The font is served from this site, and every library is vendored locally. Two kinds of request leave your browser, and only when you ask for them. The first is the AI provider call described above, which happens only after you enter a key and send a message. The second is the transcription model: the first time you transcribe audio, your browser downloads the Whisper model (about 44 MB) from Hugging Face, and keeps it in the browser cache for next time. That request is a download only. No audio, transcript, or key is sent to Hugging Face or anywhere else, and the transcription itself runs on your device.

Links on the site go to other sites (Devpost, GitHub, and the AI providers' key pages). Those sites have their own privacy policies; DeckCast does not control them.
